Trust → autonomy — earning the right to act

The autonomy ladder

Each rung earns the next · gated on precision
  • Observe silently — shadow mode, 2 weeks done
  • Surface to the lead — weekly digest, high-precision flags you are here
  • Surface to the team — ICs self-link their own work
  • Act on the low-risk, reversible slice — auto-link, auto-nudge
  • Higher-stakes actions stay human-approved — always
